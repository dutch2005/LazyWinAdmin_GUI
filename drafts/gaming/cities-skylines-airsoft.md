# From Virtual Megacities to Real-World Tactics: Cities Skylines 2 & Airsoft

When I'm not writing code, configuring cloud environments, or spending time with family, I have two very different ways to unwind. One involves meticulous planning and resource management from a god-like perspective. The other involves running through the woods, adrenaline pumping, trying not to get shot by a plastic BB.

## The Architectural Zen of Cities Skylines 2

Cities Skylines 2 has been my go-to for relaxing strategy. There is something deeply satisfying about optimizing traffic flow in a virtual metropolis. It actually taps into the same part of my brain that enjoys optimizing server architecture.

- **Traffic Flow = Network Routing:** Managing intersections and highway ramps in CS2 is shockingly similar to managing network traffic and load balancing.
- **Resource Management:** Making sure your industrial zones have access to raw materials feels just like managing database connections and memory allocation.

## The Tactical Rush of Airsoft

On the complete opposite end of the spectrum is Airsoft. Sitting at a desk all day means I need a physical outlet, and hitting the gym sometimes just doesn't cut it.

Airsoft provides the perfect mix of physical exertion, teamwork, and tactical thinking. It's real-time problem solving under pressure.

- **Communication is Everything:** Just like in a dev team, if you aren't communicating with your squad in Airsoft, you're going to lose.
- **Gear Matters (But Skills Matter More):** You can buy the most expensive loadout, but if you don't know how to move and communicate, it won't save you. The same applies to tech stacks.

Whether I'm managing a virtual city of a million people or coordinating a flank maneuver on a Sunday afternoon, these hobbies keep me grounded and ready for the next IT adventure.
